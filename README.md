- Connect over ENet and no longer over TCP ([design](plans/transport.md)): every connection is an ENet 1.3.18 link (`vendor/enet-1.3.18`, as published) to the server's port number as a UDP port (`platform/net_enet.c` is the socket interface on every platform), so a lost packet is resent after about a round trip; the TCP sockets and the original's check of Winsock's `send` and `recv` are removed, a server that has not answered a connect after five seconds reads as closed, and the client connects only to a server that speaks ENet (the ring-buffer gateway), not to a plain Ragnarok server; an input stamp is queued and leaves in one write with the request it stamps.
- Send what the player asks for through a delivery piece and no longer on the world channel ([design](plans/delivery.md)): a move, an attack, sit and stand, a stop of the attack, a skill and an intent, each with its input stamp, leave as one ordered message of `delivery/delivery.c` on a fourth ENet channel, are sent again every 15 ms until the server has them and reach it once and in the order sent, in one order with everything else the client sends, so a lost datagram costs a request a frame and not ENet's resend timer; the piece is the gateway's own source (`tools/check_delivery_copy.py` in `build.bat check`), and on a link without that channel the requests go on the world channel. The units' records and the hit notices come down through the same piece: each record carries a revision (`ZC_TRACK_MOVE`, `ZC_TRACK_ACT` and `ZC_TRACK_VITALS` are 2 bytes longer), the client merges a track's records in any order and keeps the newest (`world/move_track.c`, `world/act_track.c`), and a record that comes before its actor waits for it (`world/record_wait.c`).
- Order a unit's coming and going with its records ([design](plans/presence.md)): `0x0f26` (`ZC_VIEW_STAMP`, 17 bytes, the project's own) comes directly before every entry and vanish packet with the server tick the unit is in view from or out of view from and the revision of each of its tracks then; it counts only for the very next packet, and a vanish without one (a player's death) is no going; an act record sent later than it was made (the one with an entry) has the top bit of its kind set and is played at once, unmeasured; the floors of a unit's tracks and the ticks of its last coming and going are kept by unit id and outlive its actor (`world/view_floors.c`), every record is filed against them wherever it comes from, and one whose unit is out of view waits (`world/presence.c`), so a record made before a unit went that arrives after it came back is the past, one made after it came back that overtakes its going is kept for the later stay, and a unit that went loses its items in the act and HP queue; the predicted world has another body in the worlds of the ticks before its going, dated by the server, whatever arrived first, and a going it is already past makes it again (`world/predicted.c`).
- Send and read on three channels of that link, so a packet lost in one kind of traffic does not hold back another: the world's packets on one; whisper, party, guild and server-wide text, guild emblem images, friends, mail, the quest log and rankings on a second (46 ids this client sends, `contents/rag_connection.c`); and the timed request `0x0360` on a third that is never resent. Packets of one channel keep their order, packets of different channels have none, and the packets themselves are unchanged.
- Log in over one connection: where the original closes its connection after the account server, the login server and the character server and opens the next, the client keeps the link (`connection_hop`) and goes on over it when the next server has the same address and port, with the same packets; returning to character selection, logging out and changing zone server still close it.
